## 1. The base rate a trader starts from

- Barber, Lee, Liu and Odean studied Taiwanese day traders from 1992 to 2006 (published in the *Journal of Financial Markets*, 2014). They found that day trading loses money on net and most day traders lose money. Fewer than 1% earn reliably positive returns, and that small group does persist. [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT: Berkeley working paper](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/day%20traders/Day%20Trading%20Skill%20110523.pdf), [SECONDARY: CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/individual-investing/do-day-traders-make-money/)
- Harvey, Liu and Zhu, *RFS* 2016, argue that because so many factors have been tested, a new one needs a t-statistic above about 3.0, and that most claimed findings in financial economics are likely false. [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v29y2016i1p5-68..html)
- Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado and Zhu introduced the Probability of Backtest Overfitting (PBO), estimated by combinatorially symmetric cross-validation (CSCV). Bailey and López de Prado's Deflated Sharpe Ratio corrects a Sharpe ratio for selection bias across many trials and for non-normal returns. [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT PBO](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253), [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT DSR](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551)

**Consequence for this repo.** The default answer to "does this setup work?" is *no evidence yet*. That is what the existing n≥30 floor, pre-registration and write-once outcomes already encode. The plan adds walk-forward testing, PBO/DSR and costs before any rate is shown.

## 2. ICT, SMC, auction market theory and Wyckoff

None of these four has peer-reviewed evidence of a predictive edge that we found. They are practitioner frameworks. The value for this repo is that parts of each can be **defined objectively and then measured**, which the structure engine already does for some of them. Below, each concept gets a codable definition, what stays ambiguous, and the evidence.

### ICT and SMC

| Concept | Objective definition that can be coded | Ambiguous | In repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liquidity / sweep | A wick through a named level (PDH, PDL, swing high or low), then a close back inside within m bars | Which levels count; what m is | `structure/sweep.ts` |
| FVG | Three bars where bar 1's high is below bar 3's low (bullish), or the mirror (bearish) | Minimum size; whether it must form during displacement | `structure/fvg.ts` |
| iFVG (inversion) | A close through the far edge of an FVG; afterwards the gap is treated as acting in the opposite direction | "Close" vs "body"; how long the inversion stays valid | `fvg.ts` lifecycle |
| Order block | The last opposite-colour candle before a displacement that breaks structure | Which candle; whether to use the wick or the body range | not built |
| Breaker | An order block that fails (price closes through it) and is then retested from the other side | Same as order block, plus the retest tolerance | not built |
| Displacement | Candle body ≥ k × ATR(n) | k and n | `structure/displacement.ts` |
| BOS / CHoCH | A close beyond the last confirmed swing, in the trend direction (BOS) or against it (CHoCH) | The swing definition (N bars each side) | `structure/structureShift.ts` |
| Premium / discount | Above or below the equilibrium (EQ, the 50% level) of the dealing range | Which swings bound the range | `structure/range.ts` |
| Inducement | A minor swing that gets swept before the real move | "Minor" is undefined; usually labelled only in hindsight | not built (hard to define without hindsight) |
| Killzones / Silver Bullet | Fixed ET windows, e.g. 10:00–11:00 for the AM Silver Bullet | The window times differ by source | ticket session profiles |
| PO3 / AMD | Accumulation, then manipulation (a sweep), then distribution (expansion), within one session | Where each phase starts and ends | not built |
| CISD | A close through the open of the candle run that made the extreme | Which candles count as the run | not built |
| SMT | One correlated index makes a new high or low and the other fails to, using time-aligned confirmed swings | Pair choice; how closely times must align | `structure/smt.ts` |

**Evidence.** We found no peer-reviewed study. The vendor backtests we found are mostly negative or uncheckable [SECONDARY/SEARCH_ONLY, by a vendor that sells backtesting software]:
- A 15-minute NAS100 FVG rule from 2016 to 2026 returned about +38% against about +527% for buy-and-hold. [Backtrex](https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/ict-fair-value-gap-nasdaq)
- An FVG retest inside the GBP/USD New York AM killzone showed no edge once costs were included. [Backtrex](https://backtrex.com/en/backtests/ict-kill-zone-fvg-gbp-usd)
- A sweep + displacement + FVG study on 1,247 trades was published, but its results could not be checked here. [Backtrex](https://backtrex.com/en/blog/liquidity-sweep-strategy-backtest-results)

Treat every ICT/SMC rule as a **hypothesis to pre-register**, not as an edge.

### Auction market theory, market profile and volume profile

These definitions can be coded exactly from bars, provided volume is present:
- **POC**: the price bin with the most volume (or the most TPOs).
- **Value area**: the smallest contiguous set of bins around the POC that holds 70% of the volume. The 70% comes from a normal-distribution analogy and has not been tested.
- **Initial balance (IB)**: the range of the first 60 minutes of RTH.
- **Single prints**: TPO bins touched in only one period.
- **Initiative vs responsive**: trading that moves away from value vs trading that returns toward it.
- **TPO**: one letter per 30-minute period at each price touched.

**Ambiguous:** the bin size; whether to build from TPOs or from volume; whether to include ETH; and day-type classification.

**Evidence.**
- A vendor (ATAS) summarises a 2012 Firich analysis: price breaks the IB on most days, and nearly 80% of the time after a breakout the previous day. That is a secondary source, and it notes that the breakout definition is unclear. [SECONDARY: ATAS](https://atas.net/blog/the-auction-market-theory/)
- One account says Steidlmayer himself conceded that forecasting from day types was not possible. [SECONDARY](https://atas.net/blog/the-auction-market-theory/)
- We found no peer-reviewed evidence.

**Repo fit:** high. Profiles, value areas and IB are deterministic over the bars the setup engine already imports. Volume is optional in `Bar`, so a TPO profile is available even when volume is missing.

### Wyckoff

What can be coded:
- **Trading range**: a box between the selling-climax low and the automatic-rally high.
- **Spring / upthrust**: a sweep of the range edge, then a close back inside. This is the same detector as an ICT sweep.
- **Effort vs result**: volume relative to the bar's spread. For example, volume z-score above 2 with spread below its median is a divergence.

**Ambiguous:** where phases A–E begin and end; identifying the climaxes; the "composite operator" (an unobservable narrative device).

**Evidence:** we found almost nothing peer-reviewed. One low-tier journal paper exists, and the indicator accuracy figures in circulation are self-reported by script authors. [SECONDARY](https://evidencefinance.neocities.org/wyckoff)

### Synthesis

ICT's "sweep and reclaim", Wyckoff's "spring" and AMT's "failed auction / look above and fail" are **the same measurable event**: a probe beyond a reference level that fails back inside. The engine should define it once, version it, and let each school's label point at the same detector. It should not keep three slightly different copies.

## 3. Strategies with published evidence

No strategy listed here is reliably profitable. "Evidence" means peer-reviewed or primary work exists. Decay and costs are why most of these weaken in practice.

| Strategy | Evidence | Quality | Decay / costs / capacity | How to test here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Time-series momentum (trend) | Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen, *JFE* 2012: 58 futures, 1–12 month persistence that partly reverses later, performs best in extreme markets. [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:104:y:2012:i:2:p:228-250) | High, replicated | Weaker since about 2010 in some samples; vol scaling matters | Needs daily futures history (paid, or user import). |
| Intraday market momentum | Gao, Han, Li and Zhou, *JFE* 2018: SPY's first half-hour return predicts the last half-hour (R² 1.6%). [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:129:y:2018:i:2:p:394-414) | High | Small per-trade edge; cost-sensitive | Testable on imported SPY 1-minute bars **at $0**. |
| Hedging demand / dealer gamma | Baltussen, Da, Lammers and Martens, *JFE* 2021: intraday momentum across 60+ futures, stronger when dealer gamma is negative. [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:142:y:2021:i:1:p:377-403) | High | Needs a gamma estimate; ours is modelled from delayed open interest | Cboe GEX sign plus imported bars: a conditional version of the row above. |
| Opening-range breakout | Zarattini and Aziz, SSRN 2023: QQQ 5-minute ORB; reported CAGR assumes no spread or slippage. [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=4416622) One independent replication found the gross edge reproduces and the net is about zero. [SECONDARY](https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/776235) | Medium (working paper) | Cost and slippage dominate | Imported bars at $0, with costs that have no default. |
| Volatility risk premium (option selling) | Carr and Wu 2009; Bondarenko on CBOE PUT: implied volatility exceeds realised on average, put-writing beat put-buying over 1986–2018, with rare large losses. [SECONDARY: Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/white-paper-shows-volatility-risk-premium-facilitated-higher-risk-adjusted-returns-for-put-index) | High (premium exists); a carry trade with crash risk | Lagged the S&P TR in 2006–2015 [SECONDARY: CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/equity-options/performance-of-cboe-putwrite-indexes) | Needs an options price history (paid). |
| Order-flow imbalance | Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov, *J. Fin. Econometrics* 2014: best-quote OFI explains short-horizon price changes linearly. [PRIMARY-ABSTRACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402) | High (explanatory, contemporaneous) | Explains rather than predicts; needs L1/L2 quotes | Needs a quote feed (paid). |
| Post-earnings drift | Bernard and Thomas; the decline is documented, and Martineau 2021 finds it largely gone since about 2006 for large stocks. [SECONDARY: Quantpedia](https://quantpedia.com/50-years-in-pead-research/) | High (and so is the decay) | Largely arbitraged away in large caps | Low priority. |
| Mean reversion / VWAP | Large practitioner literature; no single primary source read here | E (not verified) | Regime-dependent | Imported bars; pre-register before testing. |
