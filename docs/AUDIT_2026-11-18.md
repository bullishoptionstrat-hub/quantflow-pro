## Findings, ranked

1. **MEDIUM — fixed. Replay outcome gaps missed the first minutes after ACTIONABLE.**
   `backend/src/research/setupOutcome.ts` counted gaps only between graded bars,
   so bars missing between the ACTIONABLE `knownAt` and the first graded bar were
   reported as `gapMinutes: 0`. That is the window where a fill and an early stop
   happen. Now counted. Test: `setupOutcome.test.ts` "missing bars straight after
   ACTIONABLE…" (fails before the fix with 0; a `- 1` off-by-one mutation also
   fails it).
2. **LOW — fixed. `docs/PROGRAM_STATUS.md` had stale rows.** "Outcome grading
   NOT STARTED" (it exists for replays), "setups-page PR not merged" (merged), a
   handoff naming `/setups` as next work, and no row for `/structure`, the grid,
   the validator, journal CSV or the ticket snapshot.

## Checked and found sound

- `/api/setups/*` is `rateLimiter(10/min)` → `requireAuth` → router-local JSON
  parser with its own cap; an unauthenticated body is never parsed. Not on the
  demo tier.
- CSV writers (`replayCsv.ts`, `journalCsv.ts`): formula-leading text
  (`= + - @`, tab, CR) is prefixed with `'` before quoting; absent numbers are
  empty cells; `#` lines strip newlines.
- No `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` anywhere in `frontend/`; user text renders through
  React escaping.
- Outcome grading reads only bars opening at or after ACTIONABLE `knownAt`;
  same-bar touches are `AMBIGUOUS_SAME_BAR`; no rate below 30 decided.

## What remains (not fixed, stated)

- Outcome grading assumes a fill at the trigger close; a next-bar gap through
  the stop is graded −1R although the loss is larger. Modelling slippage there
  is a definition change (needs a rules decision).
- `AMBIGUOUS_SAME_BAR` ideas are outside the hit-rate denominator. Excluding
  them is a choice; counting them as losses is the conservative alternative and
  is the operator's call.
- CSV guard does not treat leading whitespace before `=` as a formula; common
  spreadsheets read such a cell as text, unverified across all of them.
- The accessibility/keyboard pass across desk pages was not done in this round.
